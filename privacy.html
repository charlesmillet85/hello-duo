Hello Duo — Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Hello Duo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app uses

Hello Duo reacts to your iPhone Duo's hinge (when you unfold or fold it) and, if you turn it on, to unlocking your iPhone. It uses this on your phone only, to play a line at that moment. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your settings

Your character, how often it speaks and its times of day stay on your phone.

Purchases

The "Unlock everyone" purchase is handled by the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Voice lines

Every line is a pre-recorded audio file inside the app. Nothing you say or do is recorded.
