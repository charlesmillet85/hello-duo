Hello Duo

Hello Duo gives your iPhone Duo a personality. Pick a character, and it says a short, funny line out loud when you unfold your phone, fold it, or even unlock it.

Frequently asked questions

It doesn't speak when I open my phone.
Switch a character on with the button on its card, and keep Hello Duo running in the background. It only speaks now and then by default: in Settings › When it speaks › How often, choose "Every time" to hear it every time.

Can I choose when it speaks?
Yes: how often, on unfold, fold or unlock, and only at certain times of the day (Settings › When it speaks).

I bought Unlock everyone on another device.
Open the unlock sheet and tap Restore purchase. With Family Sharing, your family can use it too.

Does it work on other iPhones?
It's made for iPhone Duo. On other iPhones, it speaks when you unlock.
